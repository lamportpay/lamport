# Social content queue

Ready-to-post assets in this folder, in posting order. Files are served from
`https://raw.githubusercontent.com/lamportpay/lamport/main/media/<path>`.

Style rule: product-UI look only (dark brand theme, real app screens, real partner logos) or cinematic
live-action clips (Google Flow). No cartoon illustrations.
Format rule: TikTok gets 9:16, X and LinkedIn get 16:9 or images/decks.
Mark TikTok and X video posts as AI-generated. Times are PKT.

## Weekly rhythm (why each slot exists)

| Day | Channel and time | Content type | Goal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | LinkedIn 13:30 | Founder post (Zoaib) or product deck | Trust with partners and investors |
| Tue | X 18:00 · TikTok 19:30 | Cinematic hook video | Reach new people |

| Thu | LinkedIn 13:30 · X 18:00 | Educational deck (cost, custody, corridors) | Saves and shares → profile visits |
| Fri | LinkedIn 13:30 · TikTok 19:30 | Product walkthrough clip | Early-access signups |
| Sat | X 18:00 · TikTok 19:30 | Corridor story (Dubai → India, London → Manila…) | Diaspora audiences |
| Sun | X 21:30 | Week recap + early-access call to action | Convert the week's reach |

Every post ends with one call to action only: "Join early access at lamportpay.com" (first 10 pay zero LamportPay fee).
