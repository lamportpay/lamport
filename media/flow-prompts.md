# Google Flow prompt pack

Cinematic live-action clips for LamportPay ads. LamportPay adds the voiceover, the real app screen,
the route map, captions and the end card afterwards, so every prompt keeps phone screens blank.

Generate every shot twice, framed separately (never crop one into the other):
- **Vertical 9:16** for TikTok, Reels and Shorts: one subject, close framing, action in the centre third.
- **Widescreen 16:9** for X and LinkedIn: wider framing, subject on a third, room for text on the other side.

Add these lines to every prompt:
> Cinematic, natural light, shallow depth of field, 1080p, 6 seconds. Phone screen is blank and glowing softly, no text, no logos, no app names. No on-screen text.

Upload the clips here; they are cut into one ad per story in both formats.
