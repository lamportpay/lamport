## Short directory description

LamportPay, built by UK company Lamport Pay Ltd and led by founder and CEO Zoaib Ali, connects crypto on Solana to local bank payouts through Stables. The platform is non-custodial. Bank payouts and hosted identity verification remain sandbox-only pending production approval.

## Extended business description

LamportPay is the payment-platform brand of Lamport Pay Ltd, UK company number 11464834. The company was incorporated on 16 July 2018 and adopted its current legal name on 14 September 2026. Zoaib Ali is Founder & CEO and an active director. The registered office is 7 Bell Yard, London, WC2A 2JR; the business operates online only.

LamportPay is developing a non-custodial crypto-to-local-bank payment experience. Customers retain control of their wallets; LamportPay holds no customer funds, private keys or balances. Stables is the publicly identified payout infrastructure provider. Wallet sign-in and live swap quotes are distinct from sandbox bank payouts and hosted identity verification. Production payouts are not claimed. Registration at Companies House is not a financial-services licence, financial endorsement or approval to move real funds.
